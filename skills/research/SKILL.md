# Research skill

- Clarify the question and break it into verifiable subclaims.
- Prefer primary, recent, and independent sources; distinguish facts, estimates, and interpretation.
- Record source URLs or file paths and reconcile contradictions rather than hiding them.
- Keep private data and access tokens out of notes and final answers.
- End with a concise synthesis, evidence quality, uncertainty, and recommended next step.
